# F-M2-3 — Reference corpus contains an undocumented second scene-naming family

**Classification:** dataset provenance ambiguity / population-composition
uncertainty.

**Status: UNVERIFIED — an open question, not an assertion.**

**Accounting rule.** This is **not** a pipeline-defect class. It is an external
reference-corpus limitation *discovered by* the pipeline, and it is counted
separately from pipeline defects in any later failure-type analysis. Grouping it
with F-M1-1..3 or F-M2-1..2 would inflate this project's defect count with
someone else's corpus, and would understate what the audit method actually did
here — which was to surface a property of a widely used dataset that its own
documentation does not state.

## Reviewer's record

Quoted exactly from the accepted external methodological review. Programme
additions are in the next section, beside this block and never inside it.

> **Observed:**
> - 26 of 53 reconstructed scenes use the sim* naming family.
> - 1,040 of 3,696 trajectories belong to that family.
> - sim* uses a different suffix convention from no*.
> - traj_data.pkl contains position and yaw only.
> - no authoritative source located defines the prefix.
>
> **Not established:**
> - that sim means simulated;
> - that the two families differ in acquisition modality;
> - that either family should be excluded;
> - that the naming family corresponds exactly to a building, map, or robot.
>
> **Decision:**
> - retain both families;
> - stratify the scene-grouped holdout by naming family;
> - report composition;
> - prohibit modality claims without further evidence.

## Programme additions

Two supplementary observations, contributed by this programme and attributed as
such. Neither is part of the reviewer's block above, and neither establishes
anything the "Not established" list excludes.

1. **A numerical correspondence, recorded as observation only.** Public
   documentation describes GO Stanford 2 as collected across 27 buildings, and
   the `no*` family contains exactly 27 scenes. This is a correspondence, not an
   inference: it does not establish that `no*` is the building set, that `sim*`
   is therefore something else, or that either family maps one-to-one onto a
   building.
2. **Search scope for the prefix definition.** No authoritative definition was
   located in the upstream repository or in public GO Stanford documentation.
   Recorded so the absence is a searched absence rather than an unexamined one,
   and so a later reader knows where *not* to look again.

## Resolution path

Not a blocker for G0.c — both families are included either way — but the
question is on file before the split commits, and the split manifest cites it.

**Image-level observation (demoted, retained).** Sampling frames from both
families and archiving a labelled contact sheet remains legal acquisition-time
observation: JPEGs only, no checkpoint, no forward pass, no metric. Its output
is archived as **unclassified visual observations with no modality vocabulary
attached**, and nothing in this finding cites it as evidence of simulation. It
can show that frames differ; it cannot establish why.

**Documentation probe.** The GO Stanford lineage runs through Hirose et al.
(GONet and the deep visual MPC work), whose pipelines used Gibson-simulator
environments reconstructed from building scans. Those papers' dataset
descriptions, and Hirose-adjacent repositories, are the place a composition
statement would live. The finding graduates from question to defect only on such
a statement, not on inspection of pixels.

## Why it matters to G0.c

The historical anchor `avg_total_test_loss = 0.17398` was computed over a
mixture that included this corpus in its entirety. If the corpus's composition
is undocumented, then the population behind the anchor is undocumented too — and
that is a further reason, independent of F10, why the anchor is a provenance
anchor rather than a reproduction target.

The split therefore includes both families and preserves their composition in
both partitions, so that whatever the families turn out to be, the evaluation
population is *described* rather than assumed.
