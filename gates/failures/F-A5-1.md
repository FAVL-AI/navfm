# F-A5-1 — Two mechanistic prose claims frozen into a preregistration unverified

**Classification:** unverified causal/mechanistic prose relayed from subagent
summaries into protocol text. Caught by trigger-3 before commit.

**Authorship acknowledged.** Introduced by the assistant. Both claims came from
a fan-out archaeology workflow, were written into Amendment 5, and were never
independently checked by the author who wrote them.

## The two claims, and what is actually true

**1.** *"the traceability matrix referenced a ViNT gate artefact that did not
exist."* **False.** `grep -rn vint_gate` over the fork returns nothing (exit 1);
`docs/reproduction/pack/TRACEABILITY.md` names `m1_family/gnm_gate.json`, with
its next rows reading "same" and inheriting `--model gnm`. No traceability
document ever referenced a ViNT gate.

**2.** *"the historical machine-readable gate artefact was gitignored."*
**False, by conflation of two different files.** `experiments/_evidence/` is
ignored (`.gitignore:92`) and holds the *checkpoint-verification* record.
`experiments/validation/m1_family/` is **not** ignored and **is tracked** at
`900c418`, carrying `gnm_gate.json` and `nomad_gate.json`.

## Why the second one matters more than a wrong fact

It contradicted the disposition it was written to support. ViNT R7 = `RERUN`
draws its entire evidential force from a control: the gate directory is tracked,
and ViNT alone has no file in it. If gate artefacts were gitignored, ViNT's
absence would prove nothing. The sentence destroyed its own load-bearing
argument, and it sat two paragraphs from the disposition it undermined.

The author had personally verified that control earlier in the same session and
still wrote the contradicting sentence, because the sentence arrived from a
different source than the verification.

## The generalisation

Check 10 was written to catch **numbers** copied from reviewers without
recomputation — the Amendment 4 lesson. It worked: every numeric claim
reproduced. Both failures were **prose**.

> Preregistration review must independently verify mechanistic statements —
> "this file was referenced", "this artefact was ignored", "this gate covered
> this model" — with the same rigour as numeric claims. A wrong mechanism
> changes the protocol exactly as much as a wrong number, and is harder to
> notice because it carries no digits to recompute.

Related: [[F-M2-4]] (checks that cannot fail), Amendment 4's rule that a
reviewer's statement is evidence to verify. This extends that rule from figures
to mechanisms.

## Cost

One adjudication round. Nothing was committed; the gate held.
